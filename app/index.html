      ROADMAP TO THE PLATINUM — ABOUT 200 HOURS, DIFFICULTY 4/10

      One playthrough, no mission replay, no New Game+. Cheats disable trophies and saving. Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter (15 manual slots + 1 autosave).

      1. Stage 1 — Story + the one missable (60–80 h)

        Play Chapters 1–5 in order. Every story trophy is automatic and cannot be missed. Do the Chapter 1 house meditation for Positive Mental Attitude right after the mission Down to Earth — talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony, choose Accept. Save at every chapter boundary.

      2. Stage 2 — Strangers & activities

        Finish the optional questlines (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret), the 8 counting Strangers & Freaks strands, shooting ranges, drone races, fishing, arcade games and 50 challenges. Most of this happens naturally while you play.

      3. Stage 3 — Collectibles & exploration

        Scan 200 of 277 vehicles and study 100 of 255 animals for 100%, WAiNK 100 locations, 250 stunts, 3 card sets, 8 garages, and unfog all 52 areas of Leonida from a plane.

      4. Stage 4 — Robbery, combat & misc, then 100% + platinum

        Bank vaults (5 for the trophy, 8 for 100%), gang operations (1 per gang, 39 for 100%), armored trucks, jewelry stores, the plane, the tank, the UFO easter egg and the money trophies. Then finish the 100% checklist for A Long Road — the platinum pops with it.

      MISSABLE & SAVES — READ THIS FIRST

      ⚠ ONLY 1 POTENTIALLY MISSABLE TROPHY

      Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot

      The Chapter 1 meditation at Jason's house is only available during Chapter 1. After the mission Down to Earth, in the morning, talk to Lucia in the kitchen, follow her to the balcony and choose Accept. It raises the counter by 1 but is not marked as a spot, and it is still unconfirmed whether it is required — do it anyway. All other 17 meditation spots stay available.

      • No story trophy is missable. All 12 story trophies unlock automatically by playing the chapters.
      • No mission replay, no New Game+. Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter (10 saves), and rotate the remaining 5 slots for free-roam cleanup.
      • Cheats disable trophies and saving. Do not use cheats on a trophy run.
      • Missable content that does NOT affect trophies: some optional missions vanish, some quest givers go quiet for a while and return, some fences disappear and are replaced, a few magazines move to other spots, hourglass quests expire. None of it locks any trophy or the 100% checklist.
      • Avoid the Irredeemable criminal profile — it is permanent and makes police far more aggressive.
      • Jason/Lucia relationship status does not affect trophies.
